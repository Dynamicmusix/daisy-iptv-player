❓ Frequently Asked Questions
1. How do I get started?
Click ➕ Setup New MAC Portal, enter your IPTV provider's Portal URL (e.g. http://your-portal.com/c) and your authorized MAC address (00:1A:79:...), and click Save & Connect.
2. How does TV Remote / D-Pad navigation work?
Use the Arrow keys (Up/Down/Left/Right) and Enter/OK on Android TV, Fire TV, and Apple TV remotes, or touch on iOS & Android phones/tablets.
3. How do I transfer my portal between Mobile and TV?
Open Backup, copy your Restoration Code, and paste it on your TV or second device.
ℹ️ About Daisy IPTV (v1.0.0)

Daisy IPTV v1.0.0 provides a unified IPTV Set-Top Box environment across Android Mobile, Android TV, iPhone, iPad, Apple TV, and Web/Desktop with identical look, settings, and features.

Daisy IPTV does not provide or sell IPTV subscriptions or content. You must configure your own authorized IPTV portal details.
