About DAISY IPTV v1.0.0

DAISY IPTV

DAISY IPTV v1.0.0 delivers a Jetpack Compose for TV (Material 3 for TV) interface with complete Set-Top Box MAC portal emulation on Samsung Smart TV (Tizen), Web, Android TV, Mobile, iOS, and Apple TV.

DAISY IPTV comes with no built-in channels or subscriptions. Configure your authorized portal in Settings.
