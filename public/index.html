About Daisy IPTV v1.0.0

Daisy IPTV v1.0.0 combines a Liquid Glass Apple TV / tvOS interface with full Set-Top Box MAC portal emulation across Web, Android TV, Mobile, iOS, and Apple TV.

Daisy IPTV comes with no built-in channels or subscriptions. Configure your own authorized portal in Settings.
